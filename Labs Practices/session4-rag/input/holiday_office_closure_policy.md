# Corporate Holiday and Office Closure Policy

**Document type:** Corporate Policy  
**Applies to:** U.S. full-time employees  
**Policy owner:** Human Resources  
**Last updated:** January 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines official company holidays, holiday pay, office closures, and employee expectations during scheduled holiday periods.

## Official Company Holidays

The company observes **10 paid company holidays per calendar year**. The annual corporate holiday calendar published by Human Resources contains the authoritative dates.

Common observed holidays include:

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- One additional company-designated holiday

Eligible full-time employees receive regular holiday pay for these scheduled company holidays.

## Holiday Eligibility

Employees must be actively employed on the date of the holiday to receive holiday pay.

Employees are **not required to use PTO** when the company is officially closed for a scheduled company holiday.

## Weekend Holidays

When a company holiday falls on Saturday, it is normally observed on the preceding Friday.

When a company holiday falls on Sunday, it is normally observed on the following Monday.

The published annual holiday calendar overrides this general rule when business requirements require a different observed date.

## Working on a Company Holiday

Some production, support, and customer-facing teams may require holiday coverage.

Employees required to work on an official company holiday should coordinate with their manager. Depending on employment classification, they may receive an alternate paid day off.

## Floating Holidays

**The company does not provide separate floating holidays under this policy.**

Employees who want time off for a religious, cultural, or personal observance that is not an official company holiday should request PTO according to the Paid Time Off Policy.


## Annual PTO Allowance

For general planning purposes, eligible U.S. full-time employees receive **15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year**.

PTO is separate from the **10 scheduled paid company holidays** described in this policy.

Employees with unused PTO should consult the current PTO policy for carryover limits and other eligibility rules.

## PTO Before or After a Holiday

Employees may request PTO immediately before or after a company holiday.

Using approved PTO before or after a holiday **does not by itself make an employee ineligible for holiday pay**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many paid holidays do employees receive?

Eligible U.S. full-time employees receive **10 scheduled paid company holidays**.

### Do I need PTO for Christmas Day?

No. If Christmas Day is listed as an official company holiday for your location, eligible employees do not need to use PTO.

### Can I use a floating holiday for another celebration?

This policy does not provide floating holidays. Review the PTO policy for other available leave options.

### What if another document gives a different holiday count?

The current annual corporate holiday calendar and this Corporate Holiday Policy control for scheduled company holidays.
