# PTO, Personal Days, and Flexible Holiday Guidelines

**Document type:** Employee Benefits Guide  
**Applies to:** U.S. employees  
**Reference year:** 2024  
**Last reviewed:** November 2024

## Overview

Employees can use paid time off for vacation, personal matters, family needs, and additional time away from work around holidays.

This guide also describes flexible holiday benefits and includes holiday scheduling information for employee planning.

## Flexible Holidays

Eligible employees receive **2 floating holidays each calendar year**.

Floating holidays may be used for:

- Religious observances
- Cultural celebrations
- Birthdays
- Personal events
- A holiday that the employee prefers to observe instead of a standard company holiday

Floating holidays require manager approval and should normally be requested at least five business days in advance.

Unused floating holidays normally expire at the end of the calendar year.

## Holiday Schedule

For planning purposes, employees generally receive **8 standard paid holidays plus 2 floating holidays**, providing **10 paid holiday days** during the year.

Typical standard holidays include:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day
- Two additional company-selected holidays

> Note: Holiday schedules can change by year, office, client assignment, or business unit.


## Annual PTO Allowance

Eligible U.S. full-time employees generally receive **20 days of PTO annually** under the benefits structure described in this guide.

The annual allowance is separate from the **8 standard paid holidays and 2 floating holidays** described below.

Some employees may receive different PTO allowances based on tenure, business unit, client assignment, or employment agreement.

### PTO by Tenure

| Length of Service | Annual PTO |
| --- | ---: |
| Less than 2 years | 15 days |
| 2–5 years | 20 days |
| More than 5 years | 25 days |

Employees should consult Human Resources when another policy or employment document provides a different PTO amount.

## PTO Around Major Holidays

Employees requesting additional days off immediately before or after Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, or another company holiday should submit a PTO request.

Requests are evaluated based on:

- Business coverage
- Existing approved leave
- Customer commitments
- Order of requests

## Holiday Pay and Attendance

Employees are generally expected to work their scheduled workday immediately before and immediately after a paid holiday unless they have approved PTO.

An **unapproved absence immediately before or after a holiday may affect holiday-pay eligibility**.

Approved PTO normally does not affect holiday pay.

## Christmas Example

Christmas Day is normally treated as a paid company holiday.

An employee who wants December 24 and December 26 off may need to use PTO or available floating-holiday time, depending on the applicable holiday calendar.

## Weekend Holidays

Historically, when a holiday fell on a weekend, offices generally observed the holiday on a nearby weekday. Employees should check the applicable annual calendar because the observed date may differ by location.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many holidays do employees receive?

This guide describes **8 standard holidays plus 2 floating holidays**, for a total of **10 paid holiday days** for eligible employees.

### Do employees receive floating holidays?

Yes. Under the flexible holiday benefit described in this guide, eligible employees receive **2 floating holidays**.

### Can an absence affect holiday pay?

Potentially. An unapproved absence directly before or after a holiday may affect eligibility. Approved PTO normally does not.

### Where can I find the official holiday dates?

Check the annual holiday calendar published for your office or business unit and consult Human Resources if another policy conflicts with this guide.
