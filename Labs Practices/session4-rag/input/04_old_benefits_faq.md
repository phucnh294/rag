# Benefits FAQ Archive

**Archived reference — historical content**

## How much vacation did employees receive?

Under an older benefits structure, many employees received 15 vacation days annually.

Some long-tenured employees received additional days.

## Were holidays included?

No. Paid holidays were listed separately from vacation days.

## Can employees still rely on this number?

No. This FAQ is retained for historical reference and should not be used to determine a current leave entitlement.

Employees should use the current Employee Leave Benefits Policy for current annual leave allowances.
