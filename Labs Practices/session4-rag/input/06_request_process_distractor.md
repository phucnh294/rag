# Paid Time Off Request Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure explains how employees request paid time off.

## Submitting a Request

1. Sign in to the HR portal.
2. Open the Time Away section.
3. Select the requested dates.
4. Choose the appropriate leave category.
5. Submit the request to the employee's manager.

## Approval

Approval depends on staffing, business deadlines, customer commitments, and existing approved leave.

## Annual Paid Time Off

Employees may submit requests against their available annual paid time off balance.

This procedure does not define the annual number of PTO days a full-time employee receives.

For entitlement information, consult the Employee Leave Benefits Policy.
