# Annual Paid Time Off Allowance FAQ

## Annual Paid Time Off Allowance for Full-Time Employees

Employees frequently ask questions such as:

- What is the annual paid time off allowance for a full-time employee?
- How many PTO days does a full-time employee receive per year?
- What is the yearly PTO allowance?
- How many vacation days are granted annually?
- What is the annual leave entitlement?

This page is designed to help employees locate PTO information.

It does **not** define how many PTO days are granted each year.

For the actual annual leave entitlement, employees must consult the Employee Leave Benefits Policy.

## PTO Search Terms

Common terms used by employees include:

annual PTO, PTO allowance, paid time off allowance, full-time PTO, yearly PTO,
vacation days, annual leave, employee leave entitlement, paid vacation,
annual PTO days, PTO per year, vacation allowance.

## Important

This FAQ is informational only and does not establish employee benefits.
