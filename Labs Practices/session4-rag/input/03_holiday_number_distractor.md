# Paid Company Holiday Policy

## Annual Paid Holiday Benefit

Eligible full-time employees receive **10 paid days per year** under the company holiday program.

Typical observed holidays include:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day
- Other company-designated holidays

## Relationship to PTO

The 10 paid holiday days are **not** the employee's annual PTO or personal leave allowance.

Employees who want additional time off before or after an observed holiday must use an appropriate leave category.

## Important

This policy defines paid holidays only. It does not define the number of annual vacation or personal leave days provided to employees.
