# Employee Leave Benefits Policy

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Regular employees classified as full-time are credited with **18 paid personal leave days per calendar year**.

These days may be used for vacation, personal matters, or other approved time away from work.

Observed company holidays are separate from this allowance and do not reduce the employee's personal leave balance.

## Eligibility

The 18-day annual allowance applies to regular U.S. full-time employees after completion of the introductory employment period.

Part-time employees, contractors, temporary workers, and employees governed by separate client agreements may have different leave arrangements.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused personal leave days may carry forward to the next calendar year, subject to applicable business-unit rules.

## Example

If an eligible full-time employee takes 3 personal leave days around Thanksgiving, those 3 days are deducted from the 18-day annual leave balance. Thanksgiving Day itself does not reduce that balance if it is an observed company holiday.
