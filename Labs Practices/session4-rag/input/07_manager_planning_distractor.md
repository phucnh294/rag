# Manager Holiday and PTO Planning Guide

## Workforce Planning

Managers should review planned absences, annual PTO usage, vacation schedules, holiday coverage, leave balances, and staffing requirements.

When reviewing annual PTO plans for full-time employees, managers should ensure that critical business functions remain covered.

## Common Planning Questions

Managers may hear questions such as:

- What is the annual PTO allowance for a full-time employee?
- How many paid vacation days are available per year?
- How many PTO days should an employee plan to use?
- How many annual leave days remain?

This guide does not establish employee leave entitlements.

Managers should refer employees to the Employee Leave Benefits Policy for the official annual allowance.
