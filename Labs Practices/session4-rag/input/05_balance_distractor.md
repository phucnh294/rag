# PTO Balance and HR Portal Guide

## Checking Your PTO Balance

Full-time employees can use the HR portal to check how many PTO days they currently have available.

The displayed balance may include:

- annual leave already granted
- unused carryover
- leave already taken
- pending requests
- approved future leave
- manual HR adjustments

## Annual PTO Questions

Employees often use the portal to answer questions such as:

- How many PTO days do I have?
- What is my annual PTO balance?
- How many vacation days are left?
- What is my paid time off allowance?

The balance shown in the portal is not necessarily the employee's annual entitlement because it reflects prior usage and adjustments.

## Important

This guide explains how to view a balance. It does **not** define how many paid leave days are granted to a full-time employee each year.
