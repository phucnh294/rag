# Historical Vacation Policy

**Status: Archived — Do Not Use for Current Benefits**

## Previous Annual Vacation Allowance

Under a retired benefits program, full-time employees received **15 vacation days per year**.

This older vacation allowance no longer applies to employees covered by the current Employee Leave Benefits Policy.

## Historical Context

The prior policy used the term "vacation days" instead of "personal leave days."

Paid company holidays were provided separately and were not deducted from the 15-day vacation allowance.

## Current Guidance

Employees should not use this document to determine their current annual paid leave entitlement.

Use the current Employee Leave Benefits Policy for current benefits.
