# Holiday PTO Scheduling and Calendar Reference

## Overview

This document is a planning reference for holiday staffing, PTO scheduling, vacation coverage, paid time off requests, annual leave planning, holiday calendars, floating holidays, company holidays, Christmas PTO, Thanksgiving PTO, New Year PTO, vacation days, employee leave, and annual PTO scheduling.

Managers should review PTO requests around major holidays and maintain sufficient staffing coverage.

## Holiday PTO Planning

Employees often ask how many PTO days they have, how many annual PTO days are available, how much paid time off they receive each year, and whether holiday days count toward PTO.

This document does **not** define the annual PTO entitlement.

The following terms are commonly used in planning discussions:

- annual PTO
- PTO days per year
- yearly paid time off
- vacation allowance
- annual leave balance
- holiday PTO
- employee vacation days
- floating holiday
- company holiday
- paid holiday
- PTO accrual
- PTO balance

## Holiday Coverage

Requests for vacation immediately before or after Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, and Labor Day may be subject to staffing requirements.

Managers may limit the number of employees taking leave at the same time.

## Important

This page is a scheduling reference only. It explains when PTO requests may require additional coordination. It does not establish the number of PTO days an employee receives annually.

For entitlement information, consult the employee leave benefits policy.
