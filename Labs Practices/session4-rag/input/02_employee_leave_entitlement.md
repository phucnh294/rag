# Employee Leave Benefits Policy

## Annual Personal Leave

Regular U.S. full-time employees receive **18 paid personal leave days each calendar year**.

These 18 days are the employee's annual discretionary paid leave allowance and may be used for vacation, personal matters, or planned time away from work.

Company-observed holidays are separate and do not reduce this 18-day allowance.

## Eligibility

The annual allowance applies to regular full-time employees after completion of the introductory employment period.

Part-time, temporary, contractor, and client-assigned personnel may have different arrangements.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused personal leave days may carry into the next calendar year, subject to local rules and business-unit policy.

## Example

An eligible employee who takes 3 days off around Thanksgiving uses 3 of the employee's 18 annual paid personal leave days. Thanksgiving Day itself, when observed as a company holiday, does not consume one of those days.
