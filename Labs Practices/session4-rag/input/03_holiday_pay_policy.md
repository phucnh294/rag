# Paid Company Holiday Policy

## Paid Holidays

Eligible employees receive 10 paid company holidays during the calendar year.

Typical observed holidays include New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

## Relationship to PTO

Paid company holidays are not deducted from an employee's vacation or personal leave balance.

Employees who want extra days away from work before or after a company holiday must submit a separate leave request.

## Holiday Work

Some teams may need coverage on an observed holiday. Employees assigned to holiday coverage should follow manager instructions and applicable compensation rules.

## Note

The 10 days described in this policy are company holidays, not the employee's annual vacation or personal leave entitlement.
